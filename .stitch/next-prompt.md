The premium home page for Bruce Class, a modern college and academy. It should feel like an editorial university website designed by a top agency: calm, confident, spacious and quietly expensive, with a Swiss-grid structure, large light serif headlines, hairline-ruled lists and restrained, tasteful interactivity. Desktop, 1440px wide.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Project: Bruce Class — premium modern college & academy website with student/staff portals
- Platform: Web, responsive, designed mobile-first; generate DESKTOP first unless told otherwise
- Theme: Light, premium editorial + modern education technology; calm, assured, restrained, Swiss-structured, asymmetric but controlled
- Background: Warm Paper (#F6F4EF); alternate bands of Clean Surface White (#FFFFFF) with hairline borders; one Night Ink (#0F1522) dark band per page at most, plus the Night Ink footer
- Primary action color: Harbor Navy (#1C3B5A), hover Deep Harbor (#142C45); the ONLY color for buttons, links, active and focus states
- Accent: Heritage Brass (#A87A2E) for hairline rules only; Burnished Brass (#86601F) for small uppercase mono kicker labels; Lamplight Brass (#D2AE6A) on dark
- Text: Midnight Ink (#151B28) headings/body; Slate Muted (#5A6272) supporting text; Bone White (#F3F1EC) and Mist Gray (#A9B0BD) on dark
- Borders: Hairline Stone (#DCD7CB) 1px dividers; editorial lists start with a 1px Midnight Ink top rule
- Status: Evergreen (#2E6A4E) success, Amber Ochre (#8F6410) warning, Brick Red (#A2382C) error — always with a text label
- Display font: Fraunces (modern variable serif), light weights 300–420, tight tracking, large editorial scale; hero may italicize one emphasis word in Harbor Navy
- UI/body font: Geist; labels, dates, codes and numbers in Geist Mono; portals use Geist/Geist Mono only
- Section eyebrow: small uppercase Geist Mono kicker in brass, preceded by a short 28px hairline rule
- Buttons: subtly rounded corners (8px), 48px tall, Harbor Navy fill with white text; ghost variant with a thin stone outline; text links with a draw-in underline and a small arrow
- Cards: used sparingly; white surface, 1px hairline border, subtly rounded (8px, 14px for large panels), no shadow at rest; prefer hairline-ruled lists and whitespace over card grids
- Imagery: authentic school photography (students, classrooms, library, labs, faculty, campus) in subtly rounded frames with slow hover zoom; if unavailable, labeled duotone placeholders stating the intended shot
- Layout: 1320px max width, 12-column grid, generous section spacing (72–144px), asymmetric splits (7/5, 3/9), never a centered hero, never three equal cards in a row
- Motion: subtle and fast — content rises 24px and fades in on scroll, underline draw-ins, 1.04 image zoom, 200ms tab cross-fades; no bounce, no parallax, no glow; respect reduced motion
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible 2px Harbor Navy focus rings, 44px touch targets, semantic landmarks, labels above inputs
- Avoid: generic Bootstrap/AdminLTE look, gradients on text or buttons, glassmorphism panels, neon, emojis, stock-photo clichés, invented statistics (use [metric] placeholders), AI copy clichés ("elevate", "seamless", "unleash")

**Reference:** A local reference draft of this exact page is in `.stitch/designs/index.html` (screenshots `index.png` and `index-mobile.png`). Match its structure and tone, and improve the craft where Stitch can.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Navigation:** A fixed 76px bar that is transparent over the hero. On the left, a circular outlined monogram "B" in italic serif, the wordmark "Bruce Class" and a small uppercase mono subline "College & Academy". In the centre, the links Home (active, underlined), About, Academics, Admissions, Campus Life, Announcements, Events. On the right, a ghost "Apply" button and a Harbor Navy "Student Portal" button.
2. **Hero (asymmetric split, left-aligned, never centered):** On the left, a brass mono kicker "Admissions for S.Y. 2027–2028 are open", then an oversized light Fraunces headline "Where ambition becomes *achievement.*" with the last word in italic Harbor Navy, then a two-line Slate Muted lede, then a primary button "Start your application →" and a text link "Explore programs →". On the right, an image mosaic: a tall photo of students in a sunlit library, a smaller photo of a faculty member mentoring a student, and a white status note with a small green pulsing dot, "Enrollment open", "First-year application deadline" and "[Date to confirm]". Below both, a hairline rule and a four-column row of value props: 10 programs; Digital campus; Guided admissions; Community.
3. **Brand introduction:** A 3/9 split. On the left, the kicker "About Bruce Class". On the right, a large serif statement: "An institution built on high standards and close mentorship." in ink, continuing in muted gray with "We prepare graduates who can think clearly, work with integrity and lead in their communities." Below it, two short supporting paragraphs and an "Our story →" link.
4. **Featured announcements (white band):** The header "What's happening now" with an "All announcements →" link. On the left, a lead announcement with a 16:10 photo, a "Featured" brass pill badge, "Admissions · [Date]", a serif title and a summary. On the right, a ruled list of four notices, each with a large serif day and small mono month, a title and a one-line summary.
5. **Academic programs:** The header "Find the program that fits your future". Filter chips: All, Technology, Education, Business & Tourism, Health. A hairline-ruled program index where each row has a mono code (BSIT, BSED, BEED, BSTM, BSBA, MID, CGV), a serif program title, a small degree and duration line and a circular arrow button. The programs are Information Technology; Secondary Education (Mathematics, Filipino, English); Elementary Education; Tourism Management; Business Administration (Financial Management, Marketing Management); Midwifery; Caregiving. A sticky portrait preview photo sits to the left of the list.
6. **Student experience:** A zig-zag of two rows. "Classes where your voice is heard", with a seminar photo and a small ruled facts list. Then "Organizations, athletics and service", with a student organization fair photo on the right.
7. **Digital campus (Night Ink band):** On the left, the kicker "Digital campus", the headline "One portal for your whole school life" and a ruled role list (Student active in Bone White with its brass mono detail "Grades · Schedule · Payments"; Teacher; Registrar; Cashier), then a Bone White "Sign in to the portal" button. On the right, a browser-framed student portal preview with a sidebar (Dashboard, Grades, Schedule, Payments, Enrollment, Profile), two KPI tiles showing placeholder values and a "Today's classes" timetable list.
8. **Campus highlights:** The headline "Spaces built for learning" with circular previous/next buttons. A horizontal snap carousel of facility cards (computer laboratories, library and learning commons, health skills laboratory, gymnasium, hospitality training room) with alternating image heights.
9. **Statistics (white band):** The headline "Outcomes we measure" with a small mono note "Figures are placeholders until verified by the registrar." A four-up ruled row of large serif figures shown as "[metric]" with labels: Students enrolled this school year; Licensure exam passing rate; Graduates employed within a year; Years serving our community.
10. **Newsroom:** The headline "Stories from campus". A 7/5 magazine grid: on the left, a feature story with a 16:10 image, mono meta, a large serif title and a dek; on the right, three thumbnail stories. Below, a full-width video feature with a 16:9 poster, a round play button and "A day at Bruce Class".
11. **Events (white band):** The headline "On the calendar". A ruled agenda with three rows (Campus open house, Entrance examination, Foundation week), each with a large serif date placeholder, title, description, mono location and time, and an arrow.
12. **Call to action:** A rounded Harbor Navy panel with two faint concentric circle outlines. On the left, "Your next chapter starts here." and a lede. On the right, four numbered steps (Choose your program; Submit your application and requirements; Take the entrance exam, if required; Pay fees and confirm enrollment), an inverse "Apply now" button and a ghost "Talk to admissions" button.
13. **Footer (Night Ink):** The large serif tagline "Where ambition becomes achievement." with an underline-only email signup. Link columns School, Admissions and Portals with brass mono headings. A bottom bar with the copyright and "[Campus address] · [Phone] · [Email]".
